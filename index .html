About Grampanchayat Bhalwani

Bhalwani Gram Panchayat is a vibrant and progressive village located in Maharashtra, India. The Panchayat is committed to ensuring holistic development of the village through transparent governance, community participation, and implementation of central and state government schemes.

Established under the Maharashtra Gram Panchayat Act, 1958, our Panchayat comprises elected representatives who work tirelessly for the welfare of every resident — ensuring access to clean water, sanitation, education, healthcare, and rural infrastructure.

We take pride in our rich agricultural heritage, strong community spirit, and the collective will of our people to build a self-reliant, modern village while preserving our culture.

  • Open Defecation Free (ODF) Certified Village
  • 100% Piped Water Supply under Jal Jeevan Mission
  • Solar Street Lighting in all Wards
  • Common Service Centre (CSC) Operational
